Comparing Old Ranking vs PageRank Algorithm

📜 Old Way

Keyword & Frequency Ranking

Ranks pages by counting keyword matches in title, URL, and description. More matches = higher rank. Simple but easily manipulated.

🚀 PageRank (Google Way)

PageRank Algorithm

Ranks pages by the number & quality of links pointing to them. A page is important if other important pages link to it.

📜

Old Way (Pre-PageRank)

  • 🔤 Count keyword occurrences in title & URL
  • 📄 Match words in page description
  • 🏷️ Meta tag keyword stuffing
  • ❌ Easily gamed by spammers
  • ❌ No notion of page quality
VS
🚀

PageRank Algorithm (Google)

  • 🔗 Counts quality inbound links
  • ⚖️ Important pages pass more rank
  • 🔄 Iterative computation (damping factor 0.85)
  • ✅ Hard to game, reflects real authority
  • ✅ Basis of modern search ranking

⚙️ How PageRank Works

PR(A) = (1 - d) + d × Σ [ PR(Ti) / C(Ti) ]

d = damping factor (0.85)  |  Ti = pages linking to A  |  C(Ti) = outgoing links from Ti
